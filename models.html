All models

The models in order of publication. Each card says the idea in one line and what the model is known for getting right and wrong. Open a model in Essentials for the guided view, or Everything for the full trial table, the code, and custom designs. Every model has overview slides that say what to expect before any equations.

The table below shows which model explains which finding. To see why, run one experiment through several models at once on the comparison page.

Which model explains what

Every classic finding on this site, and the human contingency judgement findings from the streamed-trial studies, run through every model with its default settings. ✓ means the model shows the effect; ✗ means it does not. Click a mark to open that model with that experiment loaded. The table is computed from the models, and the tests fail if it falls out of date. A ✗ is not always a flaw: the card on the model's page says why, and sometimes the reason is that the shared experiment is shorter than the model needs.
