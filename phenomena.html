Phenomena

The findings the models are built to explain, on their own terms. Each card says what the finding is in one line and how well established it is. Its page gives the evidence, the papers behind it, and how the experiments that measured it were actually run, with no theory in the way. A finding without solid evidence is not worth modelling, and these pages say so where it applies.

The findings are in the order of the table that runs every one through every model. To see what the models make of a finding, open it on the comparison page.
